Sitting dates for the federal Parliament are agreed to by each House and published a year at a time by the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, so coverage is limited to the years already published.

Each year is split into the Autumn, Winter and Spring sitting blocks. Sittings held outside the published calendar are shown as Recall.

Both chambers usually sit together, which the published calendar marks with an asterisk. Sitting together is not a joint sitting under section 57 of the Constitution. The exceptions are tagged House only or Senate only, for the periods one chamber sits without the other.

Senate estimates are committee hearings rather than sittings, which is why the Senate has no sitting period during an estimates round while the House does.

The calendar is indicative. Extra sittings can be added, scheduled ones cancelled, and the whole calendar is displaced when Parliament is dissolved for an election. Verify with the official source before relying on these dates.
