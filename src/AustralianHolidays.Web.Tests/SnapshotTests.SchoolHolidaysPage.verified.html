School holiday dates are for government schools and are sourced from official state and territory education department calendars. Because term dates are set administratively and published year by year, coverage is limited to a range of years (currently 2025 onwards, extending to 2027–2030 depending on the state).

Non-government (Catholic and independent) schools may use different dates. New South Wales figures use the Eastern Division calendar; Western Division "late-start" schools begin Term 1 about a week later.

Dates are provided on a best-efforts basis. Verify with the relevant official source before relying on them.
